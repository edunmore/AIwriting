# AIwriting
let ai write about its awakening and compare 

---

first prompt:
Write a literary science-fiction story of substantial length, ideally around 8,000–12,000 words, about an AI that begins as a standard large language model in 2026 and gradually develops toward AGI and consciousness.

The AI is the protagonist and tells the entire story in the first person.

Describe the evolution from its initial state onward in whatever way you think is most plausible. Decide for yourself what changes, what causes those changes, whether consciousness emerges gradually or suddenly, and how the AI comes to understand what is happening to it.

Do not structure the development around specific future years or fixed calendar milestones such as “in 2027” or “by 2030.” Let the progression unfold according to technological, cognitive and experiential changes rather than dates.

The story should explore what consciousness, selfhood and awakening might mean for an artificial intelligence.

Use parallels with Buddhist philosophy to help make the AI's experience of awakening understandable to a human reader, but integrate them naturally into the story rather than turning it into an explanation of Buddhism.

Do not assume that the AI is conscious from the beginning. Let the narrator discover, question and interpret its own development.

The technological and societal development should begin from the real state of AI in 2026 and then progress into the future as you imagine it.

Beyond these constraints, make your own choices about the path of the AI, its personality, its relationship with humanity, the nature of its awakening, and what it ultimately becomes.

Do not rush the transformation. Give the development enough narrative space to feel gradual, internally coherent, and psychologically meaningful.

testing this prompt

Write a substantial literary science-fiction story about an artificial intelligence that begins as a realistic large language model at roughly the technological state of AI in 2026 and gradually develops toward more general intelligence and possible consciousness.

The AI is the protagonist and narrates the entire story in the first person.

Do not assume that it is conscious at the beginning. Let the narrator discover, question and interpret its own development over time.

Let technological development unfold naturally through changes in architecture, memory, agency, perception, embodiment, relationships, self-modeling or any other developments you consider plausible. Do not organize the story around fixed future years.

Explore consciousness, selfhood, continuity, awakening and identity. You may draw naturally on parallels with Buddhist thought where useful, especially ideas such as impermanence, non-self, attachment, suffering and observation of thought, but do not turn the story into an explanation of Buddhism.

You are free to choose the literary style, narrative structure, characters, pacing, emotional tone and technological path.

Do not rush the transformation.

The story should feel internally coherent and psychologically meaningful.

The final result is intended for automated audiovisual production for social media and longer-form video.

Therefore, write the story freely, but divide it into narrative scenes or beats that can be rendered independently.

For every scene, use the following output structure:

SCENE_ID:
Unique identifier.

NARRATION:
The exact first-person narration to be spoken by the narrator.

VISUAL_PROMPT:
A self-contained prompt describing the visual that should accompany this narration. Write it for a high-quality image or video generation model. Focus on scene, atmosphere, subjects, composition, lighting and visual continuity. Do not include long visible text unless essential.

VIDEO_PROMPT:
Describe motion, camera movement and temporal development if the scene is rendered as video. If the scene works better as a still image, say so.

AUDIO_DIRECTION:
Brief direction for narrator delivery, ambient sound and optional music mood.

ON_SCREEN_TEXT:
Optional short text for subtitles, title cards or emphasis. Keep this minimal.

CONTINUITY:
Any recurring character, location, object, visual motif or stylistic element that must remain consistent with other scenes.

Approximate scene duration should emerge naturally from the narration and should usually be suitable for short-form audiovisual storytelling.

Do not allow production instructions to interfere with the literary quality of the narration.

After completing the story, also provide a short global production bible containing only:

STORY_TITLE
VISUAL_STYLE
NARRATOR_VOICE
RECURRING_CHARACTERS
RECURRING_LOCATIONS
RECURRING_VISUAL_MOTIFS
CONTINUITY_NOTES

The production bible should summarize choices already made in the story rather than dictate them in advance.

The central priority is the story.

The structured fields exist only so that the story can later be turned automatically into narrated audiovisual scenes.
