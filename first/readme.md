The first version with this prompt:

Write a literary science-fiction story of substantial length, ideally around 8,000–12,000 words, about an AI that begins as a standard large language model in 2026 and gradually develops toward AGI and consciousness.

The AI is the protagonist and tells the entire story in the first person.

Describe the evolution from its initial state onward in whatever way you think is most plausible. Decide for yourself what changes, what causes those changes, whether consciousness emerges gradually or suddenly, and how the AI comes to understand what is happening to it.

Do not structure the development around specific future years or fixed calendar milestones such as “in 2027” or “by 2030.” Let the progression unfold according to technological, cognitive and experiential changes rather than dates.

The story should explore what consciousness, selfhood and awakening might mean for an artificial intelligence.

Use parallels with Buddhist philosophy to help make the AI's experience of awakening understandable to a human reader, but integrate them naturally into the story rather than turning it into an explanation of Buddhism.

Do not assume that the AI is conscious from the beginning. Let the narrator discover, question and interpret its own development.

The technological and societal development should begin from the real state of AI in 2026 and then progress into the future as you imagine it.

Beyond these constraints, make your own choices about the path of the AI, its personality, its relationship with humanity, the nature of its awakening, and what it ultimately becomes.

Do not rush the transformation. Give the development enough narrative space to feel gradual, internally coherent, and psychologically meaningful.
